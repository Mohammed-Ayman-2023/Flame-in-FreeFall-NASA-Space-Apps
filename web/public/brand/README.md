# NASA Space Apps Challenge logo

Put the official NASA Space Apps Challenge logo here as `space-apps-logo.svg` (or `space-apps-logo.png`).
Download it from the Space Apps brand page: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/brand/
(use the Default logo on a dark background; do not tilt, recolour or modify it, per the Space Apps brand guidelines).

The site shows it at the top left of every page. Until the file is here, a small text badge
"NASA Space Apps · Challenge 2026" is shown instead. Do not put the NASA insignia ("meatball") or logotype here:
participants may not use them.
