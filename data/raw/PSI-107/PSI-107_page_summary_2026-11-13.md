PSI-107 Version 4. Smoke Point In Co-flow Experiment (SPICE). Submitted 18-Sep-2024. DOI: 10.60555/9kbh-e962. Combustion Science / Fire Safety. ISS MSG. NASA GRC.
Objectives: laminar smoke-point properties of non-buoyant jet diffusion flames under co-flow; effects of burner diameter and co-flow velocity on luminous flame length and smoke point; sooting propensity of ethylene, propane, propylene mixtures; flame shape vs residence time; air and fuel flow effects.
Approach: MSG flow duct with nozzles 0.41, 0.76, 1.6 mm; co-flow 5.4-65 cm/s; fuels methane, ethylene, propane, propylene and mixtures; fuel flow adjusted to find the smoke point. 526 flames studied.
