PSI-106 Version 3. Structure and Liftoff In Combustion Experiment (SLICE). Submitted 18-Sep-2024. DOI: 10.60555/pm12-rg61. Combustion Science. Related: PSI-107. ISS. NASA GRC.
Objectives: structure and stability of laminar diffusion flames in microgravity; stabilization at the flame base; liftoff velocity limits for fuels and burner diameters; N2 dilution effects on soot and stability; flame length and radiative loss vs 1g; validate models.
Approach: fan-driven flow duct, exchangeable burner tubes, coflow air; methane, ethylene, N2-diluted mixtures; manual ignition; >4,000 high-resolution images across 13 test days.
Contacts: Marshall Long, Mitchell D. Smooke, Fumiaki Takahashi, Dennis Stocker. (Pasted by the user 2026-11-13 23:20.)
