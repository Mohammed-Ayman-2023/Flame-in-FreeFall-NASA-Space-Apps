01 · The challenge

The central idea is simple

1

NASA has conducted decades of experiments studying how fire behaves in space.

2

The results are scattered across a huge scientific database.

3

Your job is to build an intelligent platform that can understand those experiments, compare them, extract useful scientific insights, and help engineers make spacecraft safer from fire.

You’re essentially building an AI-powered scientific research assistant for space fire safety.
