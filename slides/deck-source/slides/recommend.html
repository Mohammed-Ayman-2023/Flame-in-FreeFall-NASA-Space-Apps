WHAT TO TEST NEXT

Five experiments that would close the habitat gap

1

Burn cabin solids at 28–32 % O₂ and 0.6–0.8 atm

No PSI solid-fuel burn above 22 % O₂; our model cannot score cotton, Nomex or silicone there.

2

Test smoke detectors with smoke made in habitat air

All 86 smoke tests ran at 21 % O₂ and 1 atm.

3

Measure still-air self-extinction at 24–33 % O₂

The habitat sits in ACME CFI-G’s untested gap, where burn time rises steeply.

4

Publish per-test FLEX-2 results

FLEX-2 flew up to 40 % O₂ at 0.5–3 atm, but PSI lists only its condition ranges.

5

Repeat key burns at lunar (0.16 g) and Martian (0.38 g) gravity

None in PSI. Silicone burned at 1 g, not 0 g; NASA’s FM2 work finds some materials burn more readily at lunar g.
