02 · Why does fire behave differently in space?

Fire on Earth: gravity creates buoyancy

This is probably the most important scientific concept in the challenge. When you light a candle:

1

The flame heats the surrounding air.

2

Hot gases become less dense.

3

Gravity causes the denser, cooler air to move downward relative to the hot gases.

4

Hot gases rise.

5

Fresh oxygen enters from below and around the flame.

This creates a continuous airflow. That’s why a candle flame has its familiar teardrop shape.
