06 · Feature 5 · Explicitly a challenge objective

Rank experiments by relevance

Imagine you’re developing a lunar habitat. You want experiments relevant to:

30% oxygen concentration

Pressurized environments

Solid materials

Fire propagation

Early fire detection

Your system could calculate a relevance score

R = w₁M + w₂O + w₃F + w₄A + w₅H

The weights wᵢ determine how important each criterion is.

M Material relevance

O Oxygen-condition similarity

F Fuel relevance

A Application relevance

H Hardware or experimental similarity
