The data: 13 PSI investigations plus the Saffire IV to VI NASA reports, put into one set of units, every fix logged, tables checked against the PDFs. Every number traces back to its file. On top of it, a dashboard built for the people who will use it: a crew briefing first, then the ranking, the explorer, cited answers, predictions and the gap map. Pick the lunar preset and every card recomputes. Where the data are too thin, for example cotton or Nomex, the model refuses to predict, and the Ask box says when the archive has no answer.
