SAME and SAME-R — Smoke Detection

Research question

How does smoke behave in microgravity, and how can we detect it?

SAME stands for Smoke and Aerosol Measurement Experiment. SAME-R is its successor.

On Earth, smoke rises because of buoyancy. In microgravity, smoke does not behave the same way. This changes how smoke particles travel through a spacecraft and interact with detection systems.

Why it matters

A fire detection system designed for Earth might not perform optimally in a spacecraft.
