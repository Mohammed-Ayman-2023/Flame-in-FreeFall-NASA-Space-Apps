02 · First: how does fire work?

Remove one component, and you can extinguish the fire

For example:

Removes heat

Water

Water removes heat.

Removes oxygen

Carbon dioxide

Carbon dioxide can displace oxygen and dilute the combustion environment.

Removes fuel

Removing combustibles

Removing combustible materials eliminates the fuel.

This is the foundation of fire prevention and suppression.
