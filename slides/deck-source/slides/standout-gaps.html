Scientific knowledge gaps

This could be an excellent feature. Suppose the user asks:

“Do we have enough experimental evidence to predict the flammability of a particular polymer at 30% oxygen and 10 psi?”

Your system should be able to identify

Which experiments are available.

Which conditions have actually been tested.

Which conditions are missing.

Whether the available results support interpolation or require extrapolation.

What additional experiments might be useful.

This goes beyond ordinary search and summarization.
