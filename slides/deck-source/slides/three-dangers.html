03 · Why is fire such a serious threat to astronauts?

Three particularly dangerous problems

1

Fire propagation

A fire could spread through spacecraft materials, electrical systems, and equipment.

2

Toxic smoke

Burning materials can release toxic gases and particles. In a closed spacecraft, these can threaten the entire crew.

3

Suppression difficulties

You cannot simply open a window to remove smoke or use the same firefighting methods you would use in a building.
