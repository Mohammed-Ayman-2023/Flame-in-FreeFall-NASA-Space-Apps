02 · Why should we care?

Because the behavior of fire changes

Ignition can differ

A material that is relatively difficult to ignite under normal Earth conditions might behave differently inside a spacecraft.

Persistence can differ

Similarly, a fire that would extinguish naturally on Earth might persist differently in a spacecraft environment.

This means we cannot simply take Earth-based fire safety rules and assume they will work in space.
