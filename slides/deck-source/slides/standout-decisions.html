Connect research to actual decisions. For example:

Research finding

Engineering implication

Higher oxygen concentration can increase flammability

Carefully select interior materials

Smoke behaves differently in microgravity

Design detection systems around microgravity particle transport

Suppression efficiency depends on environmental conditions

Select and test appropriate extinguishing agents

Every implication should be backed by evidence and clearly communicate its limitations.
