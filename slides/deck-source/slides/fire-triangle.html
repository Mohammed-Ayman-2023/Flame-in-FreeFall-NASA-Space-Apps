02 · First: how does fire work?

The Fire Triangle

Before discussing space, let’s understand fire on Earth. Fire is a chemical reaction called combustion. For a fire to happen, you generally need three things:

Fuel

Something that burns

Example: wood, paper, plastic

Oxygen

Supports combustion

Example: oxygen in the air

Heat

Energy that initiates and sustains combustion

Example: a match or spark
