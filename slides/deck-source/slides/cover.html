Flame in Freefall

Which microgravity fire experiments matter for a crewed habitat at 30 % O₂ and 0.69 atm, what do they predict, and what has never been tested?

Mohammed Ayman · Built on NASA Physical Sciences Informatics open data · Dashboard: [dashboard link] · github.com/Mohammed-Ayman-2023/Flame-in-FreeFall-NASA-Space-Apps

Future habitats on the Moon and Mars are expected to run at about 30 percent oxygen and 0.69 atmospheres. Fire behaves differently there, and differently again in microgravity. NASA has flown many fire experiments; this project turns that archive into answers for the habitat.
