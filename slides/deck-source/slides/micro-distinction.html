02 · Important distinction

Microgravity does not mean zero gravity

What microgravity means

Continuous freefall

It means the environment is in continuous freefall, so objects experience very little effective gravitational acceleration relative to their surroundings.

Also

Flames aren’t always spherical

Microgravity flames aren’t always spherical. Their shape depends on the fuel, oxygen availability, airflow, and experimental conditions.
