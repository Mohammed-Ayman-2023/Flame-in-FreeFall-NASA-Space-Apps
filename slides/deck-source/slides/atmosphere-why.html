08 · Why lunar and Martian environments matter

Why that atmosphere — and the problem it creates

Why?

Astronauts need to be able to transition between their habitat and spacesuits without spending excessive time breathing pure oxygen beforehand to reduce decompression sickness risk.

But

Higher oxygen concentrations can increase material flammability.

The engineering problem

How do we design materials, detection systems, and fire suppression equipment for an environment that is neither a conventional Earth atmosphere nor a spacesuit atmosphere?
