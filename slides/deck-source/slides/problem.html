THE CHALLENGE

Habitats will breathe richer air, and fire there has barely been tested

30 % O₂ · 0.69 atm

The same oxygen pressure as Earth air, but a much richer oxygen fraction: flames get more oxygen and lose less heat to nitrogen.

0 g

No buoyancy: hot gas does not rise and fresh air does not flow in, so fires spread, smoulder and go out differently.

Habitat point as set in the challenge. NASA’s proposed exploration atmospheres (10.2 psia / 26.5 % O₂, 8.2 psia / 34 % O₂) were flown in ACME BRE (PSI-20).

The key physics: at 30 percent oxygen and 0.69 atmospheres the oxygen partial pressure is like Earth, but there is less nitrogen to soak up heat, so flames burn hotter and longer. In microgravity there is no buoyancy, so the usual intuition fails. The archive exists, but it is scattered. We set out to rank it, predict from it, and map its gaps.
