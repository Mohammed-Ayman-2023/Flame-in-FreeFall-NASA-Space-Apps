04 · Experiment D · Suppression

FLEX — Flame Extinguishment Experiment

Research question

How can we extinguish flames in microgravity?

FLEX stands for Flame Extinguishment Experiment.

NASA studied the combustion and extinction of liquid fuel droplets. Researchers experimented with gases such as:

Carbon dioxide (CO₂)

Helium (He)

These gases can affect combustion by changing the surrounding atmosphere and reducing the conditions needed to sustain a flame. Researchers studied the conditions under which flames extinguish.

Why it matters

We need to know which suppression strategies work in space, and under what conditions.
