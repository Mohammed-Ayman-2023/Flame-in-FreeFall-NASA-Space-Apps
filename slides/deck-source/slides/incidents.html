Historical incidents

1967

Apollo 1

A cabin fire during a pre-launch ground test killed all three astronauts. The cabin’s high-pressure pure-oxygen environment contributed to the extremely dangerous conditions.

1970

Apollo 13

An oxygen tank explosion severely damaged the spacecraft’s service module, creating a life-threatening emergency for the crew.

1997

Mir Space Station

A fire caused by an oxygen-generating system threatened the crew and required an emergency response.
