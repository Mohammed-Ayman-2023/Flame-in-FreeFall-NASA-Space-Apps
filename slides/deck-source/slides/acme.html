04 · Experiment E · Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments

ACME

What it is

A collection of six combustion experiments

Unlike BASS, which focuses on solid materials, many ACME investigations study gaseous-fuel flames.

Examples include

Flame structure

Flame stability

Combustion chemistry

Effects of electric fields

Cool flames

Burning rates

Why it matters

ACME helps researchers understand the fundamental physics of combustion without the usual interference from buoyancy.
