BASS and BASS-II — Burning and Suppression of Solids

Research question

How do solid materials burn in microgravity?

Think about materials inside a spacecraft:

Clothing

Plastic components

Insulation

Electrical cables

Other structural materials

NASA wants to understand which materials ignite, how flames spread across them, and how airflow and oxygen concentration affect their combustion.
