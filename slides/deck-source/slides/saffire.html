04 · Experiment B · Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration

Saffire

Research question

How does a relatively large fire behave in a spacecraft-like environment?

Saffire stands for Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration. Unlike experiments conducted inside the ISS, Saffire experiments were conducted inside uncrewed Cygnus cargo spacecraft after they departed the ISS.

NASA studied

Flame spread

Material flammability

Oxygen conditions

Airflow

Fire behavior in spacecraft-scale environments

Why it matters

Small laboratory experiments are useful, but engineers also need to understand what happens when a fire occurs at a larger scale.
