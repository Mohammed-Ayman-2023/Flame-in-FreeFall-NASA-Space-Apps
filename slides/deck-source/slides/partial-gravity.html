08 · Why lunar and Martian environments matter

Your dashboard’s role — and a key limitation

A researcher could ask

“Find experiments that help us understand material flammability at oxygen concentrations approaching 30%, and identify which findings can be extrapolated to a lunar habitat.”

Your platform should retrieve relevant evidence, explain its applicability, and highlight the limitations.

A particularly important limitation

The PSI data currently does not contain partial-gravity combustion experiments, according to the challenge description.

That means you cannot claim that microgravity results directly establish what happens on the Moon.

You can identify relevant evidence and knowledge gaps, but must distinguish measured results from extrapolation.
