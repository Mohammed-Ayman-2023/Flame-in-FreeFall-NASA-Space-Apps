04 · What exactly has NASA been researching?

The three pillars of spacecraft fire safety

NASA has spent decades conducting experiments to understand three major aspects of fire safety.

1 · Prevention

How can we prevent materials from catching fire in the first place?

Example: testing whether spacecraft fabrics and plastics are flammable.

2 · Detection

How can we detect a fire early?

Example: understanding how smoke particles behave in microgravity and how sensors detect them.

3 · Suppression

How can we extinguish a fire after it starts?

Example: testing how carbon dioxide and helium affect flame extinguishment.
