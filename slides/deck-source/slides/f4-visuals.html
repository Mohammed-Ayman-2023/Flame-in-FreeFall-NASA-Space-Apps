Interactive visualizations

Researchers shouldn’t have to read hundreds of PDFs. They should be able to understand the data visually. Some useful visualizations:

A · Oxygen concentration vs. flame behavior

Investigate how oxygen concentration (e.g. 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 35%) relates to a measured combustion outcome. The actual relationship must be determined from NASA data; there is no universal linear response.

B · Experiment distribution

How investigations divide across detection, fundamental combustion, prevention, and suppression. This helps users understand the research landscape.

C · Experiment similarity

Identify investigations that share similar fuels, oxygen concentrations, airflow, experimental hardware, or research objectives — useful for finding related studies researchers might otherwise overlook.

Charts are illustrative and hypothetical — not NASA experimental measurements. · NASA Space Apps 2026
