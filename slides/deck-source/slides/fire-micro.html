02 · Why does fire behave differently in space?

Fire in microgravity

Now imagine lighting that same candle aboard the International Space Station (ISS). There is no meaningful gravitational force driving the usual buoyant flow.

Consequently

  • Hot gases don’t naturally rise.
  • The usual upward convection is greatly reduced.
  • Oxygen reaches the flame primarily through diffusion and whatever airflow is present.
  • The flame can become approximately spherical.
  • Flames can burn differently, sometimes at lower temperatures and with different combustion characteristics.
